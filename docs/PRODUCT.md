# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience is pharma-company executives and decision-makers (VP/Director level at pharmaceutical manufacturers) evaluating whether to engage a packaging consultant. They arrive with a specific, often time-sensitive packaging problem — compliance risk, a vendor issue, a cost overrun, a launch deadline — and are assessing whether Pack-Wise has the depth of hands-on experience to be trusted with it.

## Product Purpose

Pack-Wise is a specialized consultancy for pharmaceutical packaging: regulatory compliance support (FDA, EU GMP, serialization), vendor sourcing and management across rigid formats and printed componentry, artwork/design coordination, and cost optimization. The site exists to convert visiting decision-makers into booked consultations by demonstrating deep, credible, execution-level expertise. Success is a qualified visitor scheduling a consultation.

## Positioning

A specialized consultancy built entirely around pharmaceutical packaging — not a generalist agency that also does packaging. Every claim on the site should read as hands-on operational experience (32 years, named formats, named compliance frameworks), not generic consulting language.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Schedule a Consultation. Secondary CTA: none — single-funnel by design; don't introduce a competing ask.
- The line a visitor should remember after 10 seconds: Pack-Wise is the packaging consultant who has actually done this before, at scale, under real regulatory pressure.
- Belief ladder: (1) this firm is 100% focused on pharma packaging, not packaging-in-general → (2) the person behind it has decades of real, hands-on operational experience, not just advisory experience → (3) compliance (FDA/EU GMP/serialization) is treated as non-negotiable baseline, not an upsell → (4) engaging them is low-risk and confidential → (5) the next step (a consultation) is easy and low-commitment.
- Proof on hand: the homepage "Trusted By" names (PHARMA CORP, BIO-MED, GLOBAL HEALTH, APEX LABS, SYNTHESIS) are real clients, confirmed by the founder — shown as a plain, confident static list rather than a generic animated/desaturated marquee, since the prior treatment made genuine proof read as fabricated. No testimonials or case studies on hand yet; add real logos/case studies when available.

## Brand Personality

Approachable, practical, experienced. The voice should read like a seasoned advisor rather than a corporate institution — confident and precise about compliance and technical detail, but not stiff or jargon-heavy. Avoid the generic cheap-agency template feel; specificity (named formats, named regulations, named years of experience) is what should carry the premium feel, not decoration.

## Anti-references

Generic, templated "consulting agency" sites with stock-photo warmth and no operational specificity. No named reference sites provided; the current navy / bio-yellow / hex-grid direction is the accepted baseline to build on, not to be replaced wholesale.

## Design Principles

- Regulatory-first, always: compliance (FDA, EU GMP, serialization) is framed as an embedded baseline throughout, never a bolt-on or afterthought.
- Practical over theoretical: back every claim with operational specificity — named formats, named frameworks, real years of experience — rather than generic consulting language.
- Founder-led credibility: expertise is personalized through Kundanraj Shah's track record, not an anonymous "team" or faceless institution.
- Confidential, high-stakes framing: every engagement is positioned as a mission-critical brief handled with discretion.
- One funnel, no dilution: every page converges on a single CTA (Schedule a Consultation); resist adding competing secondary asks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA baseline: solid color contrast, full keyboard navigation, meaningful alt text on imagery. No unusual accessibility constraints beyond that.
